Implementation roadmap

From signing to full adoption

Two stages, each with a named owner at Bayzat. Data migration, configuration and training are covered by the one-time implementation fee.

Docs collection→Account configuration→Training→Monitoring→Sustained platform usage

Stage 1 · Account configuration

Led by your dedicated Implementation Specialist.

1
1–2 daysDocumentation meeting

We learn APSCO's policies and explain every documentation requirement.

2
APSCO-ledDocument collection

APSCO completes the Bayzat onboarding sheet, with support whenever needed.

3
1–3 daysDocument review

Every document checked for accuracy before configuration starts.

4
8–10 daysAccount configuration

Your account configured and employee data migrated.

Stage 2 · Training and adoption

Led by your dedicated Customer Success Manager.

1
4–7 daysFeature training

Live sessions on every subscribed feature, in Arabic and English.

2
2–3 daysEmployee activation

Bayzat rolled out to every APSCO employee.

3
APSCO-ledAccount adoption

Regular contact until every subscribed feature is active.

4
OngoingSustained usage

Check-ins on new features and help whenever you need it.

Bayzat-led phases add up to about 16 to 25 days, plus the time APSCO needs for the onboarding sheet. The Oracle JD Edwards integration runs alongside, on a timeline agreed with your IT team.
